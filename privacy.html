Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to BullionRates Global ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with international privacy regulations, including GDPR, CCPA, and Google AdSense partner policies.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can choose to disable or selectively turn off our cookies or third-party cookies in your browser settings. You may also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or Google Ad Settings.
